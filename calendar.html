※本堂每於清明節、重陽節特為眾信士之九玄七祖誦經祭拜。
2027年清明節為 4/5（一），重陽節為 10/8（五）。Every Qingming and Double Ninth Festival, the temple chants scriptures for the ancestors of all devotees. In 2027: Qingming is April 5; Double Ninth is October 8.

國曆日期依農曆換算，實際法會時間以本堂公告為準。農曆十二月下旬的法會落在2028年1月。Western dates are converted from the lunar calendar; please confirm with the temple. Late-12th-month ceremonies fall in January 2028.
