紐約花蓮慈惠堂由來Our History

紐約花蓮慈惠堂於1991年由何俊忠居士創辦。歷經老堂主何游阿粉女士十多年的耕耘，以及現任堂主何春燕女士的接續努力，才有今日莊嚴的道場。New York Hua Lian Tsu Hui Temple was founded in 1991 by Mr. Ho Chun-Chung. Through more than ten years of devoted work by the former head, Mrs. Ho Yu A-Fen, and the continued efforts of the current head, Ms. Ho Chun-Yen, the temple has become the dignified place of worship it is today.

創堂初期，何俊忠居士於紐約法拉盛羅斯福路開堂設教，弘揚聖命，金母賜名「紐約花蓮慈惠堂」。母娘慈恩濟世，受惠信眾口耳相傳，慕德而來的信眾與日俱增，2002年遷至法拉盛大學點大道。In its early days, Mr. Ho Chun-Chung opened the temple on Roosevelt Avenue in Flushing to spread the Mother's teachings, and the Golden Mother named it New York Hua Lian Tsu Hui Temple. As word of the Mother's compassion spread, devotees came in growing numbers, and in 2002 the temple moved to College Point Boulevard.

2007年，金母揮鸞指示「盡早興建宗教文化道場，舉辦社會公益活動」。當時立願效勞與隨修的信徒眾多，聞訊後四處尋訪建廟用地。2008年，在母娘顯靈與貴人張善良先生的因緣下，於大學點龍頭地段尋得一片空地，正是金母靈示的建廟寶地。承蒙信徒鼎力捐輸、金母神威護佑，終於購下這片土地。In 2007 the Golden Mother instructed, through divine writing, that a religious and cultural center be built as soon as possible and that the temple hold charitable activities for the community. Many devotees vowed to help and searched far and wide for a site. In 2008, through the Mother's manifestation and a fortunate meeting with Mr. Chang Shan-Liang, they found a vacant lot at a prime location in College Point — the very site the Golden Mother had indicated. With the devotees' generous donations and the Mother's blessing, the land was purchased.

2009年建堂之初，困難重重：既有外來的壓力，又有美國工程法規與申請手續上的種種難關。此時在母娘顯靈之下，建商 Peter Chen 聞訊前來響應，在他的帶領下，工程順利破土動工。擇善固執、外柔內剛的何堂主自感肩負重任，日日親自帶領師兄師姐看守廟地。她視建廟為信心的考驗，也是一份殊榮；這種為眾生創設靈修聖地而不畏艱難的精神，感動了眾多信徒，大家懷著感恩與回饋的心，承受辛苦而無怨無悔。When construction began in 2009, the temple faced many obstacles: outside pressure, American engineering regulations and a difficult permit process. Then, through the Mother's manifestation, a builder named Peter Chen heard of the project and answered the call. Under his leadership ground was broken and construction began. Ms. Ho, gentle in manner but firm in resolve, felt the weight of this responsibility and led the brothers and sisters every day in watching over the site. She saw building the temple as both a test of faith and an honor, and her determination to create a sacred place for all people, whatever the hardship, moved many devotees, who gave their gratitude and effort without complaint.

誠如母娘諭示「關關難過關關過」，在何堂主與信眾齊心努力下，興建工程終於完竣，並於2012年國曆6月13日（農曆四月二十四日）入廟，舉行　瑤池金母安座大典。本堂是美東唯一的東方建築宗教聖殿，也是啟迪世人智慧的道場，願神恩無遠弗屆，信仰功果均霑，九生十類皆受惠。As the Mother taught, “every barrier is hard, yet every barrier is passed.” Through the united effort of Ms. Ho and the devotees, construction was completed, and on June 13, 2012 (the 24th day of the 4th lunar month) the temple held the enthronement ceremony of the Golden Mother of the Jasper Pool. It is the only sacred hall of traditional Eastern architecture on the US East Coast and a place to enlighten the world. May divine grace reach far, and all living beings receive its blessing.

本堂位於皇后區大學點，坐擁都會中的絕佳福地。廟宇依台灣名師設計而建，雄偉壯觀，殿脊銅瓦覆頂，脊昂高翅，富麗莊嚴，靈氣十足。拾級而上，爐煙觸目，清香四溢，讓人一洗風塵，實有絕殊離俗之感。Located in College Point, Queens, the temple enjoys an exceptionally auspicious site in the heart of the city. Designed by a renowned Taiwanese master, it is grand and majestic, its ridges roofed in bronze tiles with soaring upturned eaves, rich, solemn and full of spiritual energy. Climbing the steps, one sees incense smoke rising and smells its fragrance — the dust of the world falls away, and one feels truly removed from the ordinary.
