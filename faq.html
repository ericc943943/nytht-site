第一次來，要怎麼拜？It's my first visit. How do I pay respects?
進廟後在祭改處左側取三枝香、點香，再依參拜順序參拜：大殿、文昌帝君殿、五路財神殿，從出口出去後在外廊主爐、前庭天公爐各插一枝香，回到室內後上二樓太歲殿、三樓三清殿。一共三枝香，右側入口進、左側出口出。Take three incense sticks to the left of the Jigai desk and light them, then follow the order of worship shown on our floor plan. Three sticks in all; enter on the right and exit on the left.
需要自己準備香、供品或金紙嗎？Do I need to bring incense, offerings or joss paper?
香：堂裡免費提供，在祭改處左側取香。
供品、鮮花：可以自行準備，建議拜完後領回。
金紙：請向櫃檯購買。Incense is provided free of charge. You may bring offerings and flowers; please take them home after worship. Joss paper can be purchased at the front desk.
我今年犯太歲嗎？Do I clash with this year's Tai Sui?
生肖與當年值年太歲相同（值太歲）或相沖（沖太歲）的人，民間習俗建議安奉太歲，祈求平安。今年的值年太歲請看本堂設施・太歲殿，點太歲燈請看點燈祈福。If your zodiac sign is the same as, or opposite to, the year's Tai Sui, tradition recommends a Tai Sui lamp. See the Tai Sui Hall for this year's Tai Sui and Lamp Lighting to register.
點燈可以幫家人點嗎？要準備什麼？Can I light lamps for my family? What do I need?
可以。一張報名表限同一住址的家人使用，每一位可以點一盞或多盞燈，每盞 $120。請準備每一位的姓名和生日，農曆或國曆都可以。Yes. One form covers family members at the same address; each person can have one or more lamps ($120 each). Have each person's name and birthday ready (lunar or Western).

點燈之後，堂裡會做什麼？What happens after I light a lamp?
凡點太歲燈、光明燈、文昌燈、財神燈的信眾，本堂於每月農曆初一、十五誦經上疏，為大家祈福。On the 1st and 15th of every lunar month, the temple chants scriptures and presents prayers for all lamp holders.
超渡法會可以超渡哪些對象？本人要到場嗎？Who can be included in the Deliverance Ceremony? Do I need to attend?
可以超渡歷代祖先、往生親友、冤親債主、地基主、嬰靈（水子靈）等，每牌位 $160。法會下午有解冤釋結儀式，報名的人需到場祭改；如果不克前往，可以準備個人衣物，請家人或朋友代為祭改。Ancestors, departed family and friends, karmic creditors, land spirits and unborn children may be included ($160 per tablet). Participants should attend the afternoon purification rite; if you cannot come, a family member or friend may bring a piece of your clothing on your behalf.
