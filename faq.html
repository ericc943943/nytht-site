來訪參拜Visiting

請問慈惠堂的參拜時間是幾點到幾點？When can I visit?
本堂每天上午 9 點到下午 4 點開放，歡迎隨時來參拜。遇到法會或過年期間，時間可能會調整，出發前可以先看看本堂公告。
每月農曆初一、十五中午備有平安齋，法會期間中午也有素食招待，歡迎來參拜的信眾一起用齋。We are open every day from 9:00 am to 4:00 pm, and you are welcome any time. Hours may change during ceremonies and Lunar New Year, so it is worth checking our announcements before you come.
A free vegetarian lunch is served on the 1st and 15th of every lunar month, and a vegetarian lunch is also offered during ceremonies; everyone who comes to worship is welcome to join.

第一次到慈惠堂參拜，請問該怎麼拜？It's my first visit and I'm not sure what to do.
別擔心，照著順序走就可以了。
進門後先到祭改處左側取三枝香、點香，接著依序參拜大殿、文昌帝君殿、五路財神殿。從出口出去後，在外廊主爐和前庭天公爐各插一枝香，再回到室內，上二樓太歲殿、三樓三清殿參拜，最後一枝香插在三樓外廊的香爐。
全程一共三枝香，記得右側入口進、左側出口出。參拜順序有圖可以對照。Don't worry — just follow the route. Take three incense sticks to the left of the Jigai desk and light them, then pay respects in the Main Hall, the Hall of Wenchang and the Hall of the Five Wealth Gods. Leave by the exit and place one stick each in the main burner on the veranda and the Heaven burner in the front yard. Come back inside, go up to the Tai Sui Hall on the second floor and the Hall of the Three Pure Ones on the third, and place your last stick in the burner on the third-floor veranda.
That is three sticks in all; enter on the right and exit on the left. Our order of worship has a floor plan you can follow.
請問參拜需要自己準備香、供品或金紙嗎？Do I need to bring incense, offerings or joss paper?
香不用帶，堂裡免費提供，在祭改處左側就可以取用。
供品和鮮花可以自己準備，供品建議以素菜為主，拜完之後建議帶回家。
金紙可以在櫃檯購買。No need to bring incense — it is free, and you can pick it up to the left of the Jigai desk.
You are welcome to bring your own offerings and flowers. We suggest vegetarian food for offerings, and taking them home afterwards.
Joss paper is available at the front desk.
請問堂內可以拍照嗎？May I take photos?
可以的，歡迎拍照留念。Yes, you are welcome to take photos.

網站右上角按「English」就能切換成英文。現場是否有人能用英文協助，要看當天值班的人員。This website is available in English (top right). Whether someone can help you in English at the temple depends on who is on duty that day.

點燈與安太歲Lamps & Tai Sui

請問怎麼知道自己今年是否「犯太歲」？How do I know if I clash with this year's Tai Sui?
生肖和當年的值年太歲相同，叫「值太歲」；相沖的，叫「沖太歲」。依民間習俗，這兩種情況都建議安奉太歲，祈求一整年平安順利。
今年的值年太歲可以到本堂設施・太歲殿查看，想點太歲燈請看點燈祈福。If your zodiac sign is the same as the year's Tai Sui, or directly opposite it, tradition suggests lighting a Tai Sui lamp for a safe and smooth year.
You can look up this year's Tai Sui under Tai Sui Hall, and read about the lamp under Lamp Lighting.
請問可以幫家人點燈嗎？需要準備哪些資料？Can I light lamps for my family? What do I need?
可以的。一張報名表可以填同一個住址的家人，每個人可以點一盞或多盞燈，每盞 $120。
報名前請先準備好每一位的姓名和生日，農曆、國曆都可以。Yes. One form covers family members living at the same address, and each person can have one or more lamps ($120 each).
Before you start, please have each person's name and birthday ready — lunar or Western is fine.
點燈之後，慈惠堂會為點燈的信眾做些什麼？What does the temple do for me after I light a lamp?
凡是點了太歲燈、光明燈、文昌燈或財神燈的信眾，本堂每個月農曆初一、十五都會誦經上疏，為大家祈福。On the 1st and 15th of every lunar month, we chant scriptures and present prayers for everyone who has a Tai Sui, Guangming, Wenchang or Five Wealth Gods lamp.

法會Ceremonies

請問慈惠堂的法會什麼時候舉行？When are the ceremonies held?
全年的法會都列在年度行事。上面的國曆日期是依農曆換算的，實際時間還是以本堂公告為準；最新消息可以看最新活動或本堂 Facebook。The whole year is listed in our Annual Calendar. The Western dates there are converted from the lunar calendar, so please check Latest Events or our Facebook page for confirmed times.
請問超渡法會可以為哪些對象報名？本人一定要到場嗎？Who can I register for the Deliverance Ceremony? Do I have to attend?
歷代祖先、往生親友、冤親債主、地基主、嬰靈（水子靈）都可以報名超渡，每個牌位 $160。
法會當天下午有解冤釋結儀式，報名的人需要到場祭改；真的沒辦法來的話，可以準備一件自己的衣物，請家人或朋友代為祭改。You may register ancestors, departed family and friends, karmic creditors, land spirits and unborn children ($160 per tablet).
There is a purification rite in the afternoon, and those who register should be there for it. If you really cannot come, a family member or friend may bring a piece of your clothing and take part on your behalf.
